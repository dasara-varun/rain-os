# Lesson 7: Multi-Screen Displays & Device Pairing

Rain OS makes managing multiple monitors and syncing with external devices seamless.

## Multi-Monitor Management
Rain OS provides native multi-monitor management with fractional scaling across Wayland and X11:
- **Per-Monitor Fractional Scaling**: In COSMIC Desktop or KDE Plasma, set your 4K primary monitor to 150% while keeping a 1080p secondary monitor at 100%.
- **Variable Refresh Rate (VRR)**: Enable FreeSync or G-Sync for fluid, tear-free gaming.
- **Display Tool**: Run `rain-display-manager gui` or open **COSMIC Settings -> Displays** to configure layout, orientation, and primary display.

## Phone Integration with KDE Connect
Link your smartphone (Android or iOS) to your PC:
1. Install **KDE Connect** from Google Play, F-Droid, or Apple App Store.
2. Make sure your phone and Rain OS PC are on the same Wi-Fi network.
3. Launch `kdeconnect-app` on Rain OS.
4. Click **Pair** to sync notifications, shared clipboard, file sharing, and media controls.
